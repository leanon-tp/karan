About Me

Hi, I'm Karan Tsai 👋

I hold nationality in both Taiwan and the United States, and I finished high school in Toronto, Canada. I studied journalism and mass communication at Ming Chuan University in Taiwan, where I wrote for the school's English newspaper and helped international students as a student affairs assistant.

Working across cultures made me curious about how countries talk to, compete with, and cooperate with each other. That curiosity brought me to South Korea, where I am now pursuing a Master of Arts at the Graduate School of International Studies (GSIS), Yonsei University.

My research interests include East Asian security, international security, international political economy, and conflict resolution. I also write for the Yonsei GSIS Newsletter, covering international affairs, embassy events, and campus life.
